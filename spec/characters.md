# Characters

## Design Principles
- 3 character types for now: Warrior, Wizard, Hunter
- Each has different play styles via stat differences

## Stats

| Character | Attack | Health | Action Points | Speed | Color |
|-----------|--------|--------|---------------|-------|-------|
| Warrior   | 3      | 10     | 3             | 2     | Blue  |
| Wizard    | 5      | 6      | 2             | 1     | Purple |
| Hunter    | 4      | 7      | 3             | 3     | Green |

## Descriptions
- **Warrior**: Strong and tough. A frontline fighter. High HP, moderate damage.
- **Wizard**: Powerful spells but fragile. Highest attack, lowest health and speed.
- **Hunter**: Fast and versatile. Strikes swiftly. Best speed, balanced stats.

## Notes
- Speed determines turn order in multiplayer (highest goes first)
- Speed also determines movement range per move action (tiles reachable = speed)
- Action Points replenish at the start of each turn
